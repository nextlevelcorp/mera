- Legal sizes (R17 research, second pass, 2026-11-20). The horse mackerel's legal minimum is now **12 cm** (was 13): Tebliğ 2026/26 (Resmî Gazete 33372, 16 September 2026, Madde 5) changed the "İstavrit (karagöz)" row of Çizelge 7, which covers *Trachurus trachurus* and *T. mediterraneus*; bluefish (18 cm) and bonito (25 cm, closed 1 April to 14 August) are unchanged. Read for R17: Spain's recreational decree (RD 347/2011, Article 4 sends to RD 560/1995: horse mackerel 12 cm in the Mediterranean, but only for outer waters; inner waters are regional) and Italy's DPR 1639/1968 (Article 87: bonito 25 cm, no horse mackerel or bluefish entry; sport rules in Articles 138 to 144). Greece is blocked (`minagric.gr` 403). Four legal questions for the owner in `spot_research.md`. 260 tests.

- Roadmap R20, baiting by hand (owner decision D9, 2026-11-19: "baiting should be a hand-skill screen, for every kind of bait"). Pressing Cast with a bare hook, a new rig or a new bait opens a short baiting screen instead of the cast: two or three steps per kind of bait (live fish: hold it, find the spot behind the back fin, push the hook through; shrimp: peel, thread from the tail, hide the point; soft plastic: line up, thread, keep it straight; a minnow: open the snap, clip it; sabiki: unwind, clip the weight). In each step a needle swings over a bar and a tap (anywhere, or the big Tap button) inside the green zone scores. The mean accuracy is the baiting quality, shown as "well done", "it will do" or "badly". It matters: a poor baiting keeps only `RIG_Q_MIN` (0.8) of the bait's draw, a badly put-on shrimp may fly off on the cast (`BAIT_FLYOFF` 0.35 at the worst) or be stolen by a fish that only mouths it (`BAIT_STEAL` 0.5), and a well-hooked live fish tires more slowly. A shrimp is now used when it goes on the hook, not on every cast, and stays on until it is lost; a lure that goes with a broken line must be tied on again. Pure `Baiting` (`game/sim/baiting.gd`), `BaitPanel`, the session's baiting state. 248 tests.

- Roadmap R9: the angler gets better by fishing.
