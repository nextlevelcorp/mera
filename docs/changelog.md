- A beach (owner, 2026-11-24: "at least one place should be a beach, not a breakwater"). The end of Maro's beach and the beach by Patras harbour are now beaches: sand (CC0 `coast_sand_01` from Poly Haven) slopes into the sea, darker where it is wet, and the wash runs up it and slides back; no rail, no lamps, the neighbours stand well apart on the slope. A fish is not lifted up a wall there but drawn out of the wash and up 3 m of sand (`LIFT_HEIGHT_BEACH_M`), and the water and the sand carry most of its weight (`BEACH_WEIGHT_SHARE` 0.3, design fill), so a fish that would part a light line up the bridge's side comes up a beach. The landing view looks down the sand to the wash. Fixes to the skylines: the shore beside the angler is kept low (0.035 rad within 2 km) instead of standing like a cliff, and the shore is left out of the sea's reflection and its haze, where its hard edges made stripes and a pale block on the water. 333 tests.
- Each place has its own skyline (owner, 2026-11-24: "every place looks the same; start with their own skylines, at sea and at the new places"). For every position the land round the horizon is worked out from where it stands: the real coast (Natural Earth 1:10m) and the real peaks within 45 km (Wikidata, with their heights), with the Earth's curvature taken off. So the Galata Bridge looks across at the Asian shore's town, Naples has Capri off its piers and Vesuvius to the east, Patras the mountains across its gulf, Sesimbra and Cascais the open Atlantic, Trieste its own waterfront along the side, and a boat sees the coast from 400 m out. Rooftops and night lights show only where the land is town; far land is hazier. Not sourced yet (design fills): the ground between the peaks, the height of the towns, and a 1.5 times lift on screen so a far shore is not a line a few pixels high. A true elevation model (SRTM or Copernicus DEM) would replace the fills: that is a data licence to approve. New `Horizon`, `tools/map/build_horizons.py`, `tools/map/fetch_peaks.py`. 329 tests.

- Roadmap R23, part 1: boat fishing by drift (owner decisions D7 and 2026-11-23: one spot per country, drift first, rent and rank as they are). The Galata Bridge, the Almería breakwater, Trieste's Molo Audace, Sesimbra and Kavala each have a rented boat (150 TL, open at Master of the bridge as before), a fourth position with `stage: "boat"` over deeper water (design fills). In a boat the lure is dropped straight down (`BOAT_DROP_M`), slack line lets a sinker fall and reeling lifts it; the drop is over when it is back at the top. The boat drifts downwind (`BOAT_DRIFT_PER_WIND` 0.04 m/s per m/s of wind, at most 0.6 m/s), so the water under it changes along its depth profile; a fast drift drags the line aslant and a light lure cannot reach the bottom (depth limit times cos(atan(drift / sink rate))); drifting passes more fish (interest up to x1.5 at 0.3 m/s). The HUD adds "drift 0.12 m/s, 14 m out". A fish comes in over the gunwale (0.8 m lift). A visit never starts in the boat. The scene is a small open boat seen over its bow (`Boat3D`), heaving and rolling with the chop; no neighbours. The map card says where a boat is for rent. New pure `BoatDrift`, `Economy.pay`. - Owner's play notes (2026-11-21), third part: the landing. When a tired fish reaches the foot of the wall the view now looks down over the rail: the bridge's side girder and a pier (or the quay's stones) drop to the water, and the fish hangs on the line, swinging and kicking, while it is reeled up the wall; a brass ruler on the wall shows how high it is. The lift is real height now: 5 m up the bridge, 1.5 m up a quay (`LIFT_HEIGHT_BRIDGE_M`, `LIFT_HEIGHT_QUAY_M`), at 1 m/s at full reel (`LAND_LIFT_M_PER_S`). The fish's whole weight is on the line, and a kick adds a surge (`LAND_KICK_SURGE` 0.8 of its weight while reeling through it, 40 % of that with the reel still, after a 0.25 s grace to react); more than the line holds parts it, so a heavy fish on a light line is lifted between kicks. At the top it is swung over the rail and the landing ends on the bucket: every fish kept this visit swims in it, the new ones drop in with a splash, and the result sits beside it with a button to put this landing's undersize fish back in the sea (they never reach the bag). - Owner's play notes (2026-11-21), second part: not every pull is a fish. While the lure or bait is down, weed now and then catches the line (`WEED_RATE_PER_S` 0.003) and a lure fished near the bottom can snag it (`SNAG_RATE_PER_S` 0.012 within `SNAG_NEAR_BOTTOM_M` 0.5 m; never under a float). Both look like a take; striking brings up a tuft of weed (the cast is over) or meets the bottom, which either lets go or parts the line and keeps the lure (`SNAG_BREAK` 0.45). A soft plastic can be torn off by a fish that is not hooked (`SOFT_TEAR` 0.12): tie on a new one. A sabiki brings up several fish at once: each of the other five hooks holds another fish of the same shoal with probability `SABIKI_EXTRA_P` (0.35) times the species' schooling, so a landing says "+2 more on the other hooks, 3 empty" and the fish go in the bag. The debug menu gets "Force weed" and "Force a snag". Fix: the first-run aim hint no longer covers an outcome message. 284 tests.

- Roadmap R20, baiting by hand (owner decision D9, 2026-11-19: "baiting should be a hand-skill screen, for every kind of bait").
