# Changelog

## Unreleased
- First real spot: the Galata Bridge (owner choice 2026-11-13) in `game/data/` with four standing positions and three species (horse mackerel, bluefish, Atlantic bonito). Sourced: coordinates (Wikidata Q81523), monthly water temperature (MGM), angler pressure and seasons (Zengin 2009), length ranges and length-weight relations (Van et al. 2019, Dürrani 2023, Bal et al. 2015, Genç et al. 2025 citing Kahraman et al. 2014), legal minimum sizes and the bonito closed season (amateur fishing communique 6/2, Çizelge 7), valid names (WoRMS). Depths, behaviour and tackle are design fills. The fishing screen now runs on `res://data`; the result says when a fish is under the legal size. 82 tests.
- Docs: `spot_research.md` (first pass at real Turkish sea spots from Wikidata coordinates, DergiPark papers, MGM temperatures, WoRMS and GBIF; recommends the Galata Bridge) and `spot_candidates_tr.csv` (287 coastal structures in Turkey from Wikidata, CC0). No game data changed.
- 90 s rule: a fish counts as landed at the cap only if it is tired and within `CAP_LAND_DISTANCE_M` (5 m) of the shore; otherwise the hook pulls (owner decision 2026-11-13). A landed fish is now swung out of the water and hangs from the line, kicking and dripping (damped pendulum), before the result shows.
- Recorded sounds: ten CC0 files in `game/audio/` (waves, wind loop and gust, reel loop, splashes, lure drop; owner listened on 2026-11-13) played by `SoundDirector`: wind follows the wind speed, waves the chop, the reel's pitch and volume follow line going out or coming in. 196 KB in total.
- CI and export: the release preset excludes `debug/*`; a second preset "Web Debug" (feature tag `mera_debug`) is exported to `/debug/` on Pages; CI fails if the release pck contains the debug menu (owner approval 2026-11-13). 76 tests.
- Fishing loop on demo data: cast with a power swing, lure flight, waiting with nibbles and takes (rod-tip spring, rings, generated cues, Android vibration), Strike, fight, result with first-catch and record notes, catch log, cast again. `WaitSim` (real-time stages 3 and 4 with the shoal follow-up; three new design-fill constants), versioned local save (`SaveGame`, `SaveFile`: log, records, last rig and position; old or corrupt saves migrate or reset with a message), rig panel with the casting-weight warning, overmatched-tackle hint (PRD F19), procedural fish body, generated sound cues, and a debug menu in debug builds only. Replaces the stand-alone fight screen. The test runner now fails a test file that does not load. 75 tests.
- Fight screen: `game/scenes/fight/` binds `FightSim` to two-thumb touch controls (left pad reel, right slider drag setting, "Çek" rod pull), a tension bar with the 80 % strain mark and strain timer, a distance readout, the line to the fish, and a result panel (landed, line broke, leader cut, hook pulled out, lost at the edge, with the 90 s rule noted). `FightController` (frame-time accumulation, 0.25 s frame cap, HUD without fish energy) and `FightSetup` (demo rig and fish size from data) are headless-testable; `WaterView` is the shader water as a reusable control. Demo data in `game/data/demo/` is all design fill. Opened from a temporary title-screen button. Drawing depth is offset by 4.6 m so the last metres stay on screen. Not in this change: sound, fish body animation, return to cast/bite flow, save, debug menu, real spot data. 60 tests.
- Water prototype: breakwater foreground, Cast button with lure flight, splash droplets and perspective rings, lure riding the shader's waves, clouds with reflections, and the line as a Verlet chain moved by gravity and wind (`game/ui/fx/verlet_rope.gd`, `splash_fx.gd`). 50 tests.
- Water prototype: second shader `water_realistic.gdshader` (ray-cast sea plane, wave normals, Fresnel sky reflection, sun and moon glitter, whitecaps, haze, far shore), now the default at 720p, with a button to switch back to the basic water. First phone measurements recorded in `design_direction.md` (worst 1% of frames: 55 fps on iPhone 16e, 57 fps on iPhone 14, basic water at 720p).
- Docs: `docs/audio_sources.md`, a shortlist of CC0 and public-domain sound candidates with source page, licence, author and measured duration; no audio committed.
- Water prototype: `game/scenes/water_prototype/` (shader water at 360p or 720p driven by chop, clarity and light; rod, line and lure overlay; fps overlay via `FrameStats`), opened from the title screen. Test scene, to be removed or gated before 0.1. 42 tests.
- Docs: owner decisions of 2026-11-12 in `design_direction.md` (test phones iPhone 16e and iPhone 14, 30/60 fps, art option A provisional, free real sounds first, no music).
- Docs: `docs/design_direction.md` proposes the performance budget (30 fps floor, 60 goal, test phone), art direction options, audio direction and the water prototype with pass criteria; includes build-size and simulation-cost measurements.
- Real-time fight: `game/sim/fight_sim.gd` (`FightSim`, fixed 30 Hz step, seeded): fish run / rest / thrash states, drag slip and line recovery, line break and strain, teeth, slack hook pull-out, landing phase, 90 s rule. Not connected to controls or a scene.

- Closed-form fight estimate corrected: a fish that can slip the drag is recovered after it tires (tire + recover), not during. Examples B and C2 changed (43 s and 60 s); `TEETH_RATE` lowered to 0.02. Workbook gained a `Cast` sheet (185 formulas). 39 tests.
