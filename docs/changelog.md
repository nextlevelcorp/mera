Four fictional anglers (Hasan, Nuri, Cemil, Ayten) keep a routine by time of day along the rails; someone who was away walks in from the nearer plaza, round the road, to their spot and fishes over the water with a rod out; two vendors (Recep, Selma) stand behind the kiosks. - Top-down world art (owner, 2026-11-17: "work on the 2D graphics"). The grey boxes are gone. The ground uses the same CC0 texture sets as the 3D view at real size: cobbles on the plazas, concrete paving on the walkways, dark rough concrete on the road, through a ground shader that samples each texture twice (turned and offset) and blends them by large soft noise, so the repeat does not show, with patches of wear. The water is a shader: the 3D sea's ripple map scrolled twice, sun glints, darker away from the edges, foam where it meets stone. A 2D sun (`DirectionalLight2D`) brings out the stones through their normal maps, the lamps light the ground at dusk and night (`PointLight2D`), and a `CanvasModulate` tints the world by time of day (switched off while fishing, so it does not tint the fishing view). Shadows fall to the bottom right from the rail, lamp poles, kiosks and the player. The rail has posts and a handrail; the lamps have two globes; the fishmonger and the tackle shop are market kiosks with striped awnings, a counter of fish on ice or of rods and lure boxes, and a sign. The player is drawn from above twice life size (shoulders, cap, stepping feet, rod carried forward), and the camera is closer (about 27 m across at 1280 px). 191 tests.
- Roadmap R9: the angler gets better by fishing. Three skills earn experience: casting (every cast, more for a release at the top of the swing), strike (every hooked fish, more for good timing) and knots (every landed fish, more when the line was loaded past the strain share). Each level above 1 adds a point to the angler attributes the model already reads: casting distance and accuracy (longer, straighter casts), strike timing (a wider strike window; the default angler keeps the model's numbers exactly) and knot strength (a stronger knot share of the line). The result panel lists the experience of that cast, a toast announces a level up, and the catch log shows each skill's level and experience. Pure `Progress` class; experience in the save as the optional `angler_xp` key (save stays version 2); constants `XP_*` and `STRIKE_SKILL_WINDOW` are design fills. Not done: spot unlocks (there is one spot), a fight skill (the fight model has no angler attribute to raise yet). 191 tests.
- Roadmap R8: the first economy, so fishing has something to aim for. **Lures are stock now:** a new player (and a save from before the shop, once) gets starting lures (6 soft, 3 minnow, 3 çapari); a broken line or a bitten-through leader takes the lure used, immediately; with none left for the rig the cast does not start ("buy more at the tackle shop"); the rig panel shows how many are in the bag. **The shops:** the Galata Bridge world has a fishmonger and a tackle shop on the north plaza. Walk up and press "Go in" (or Enter): the fishmonger lists the fish in the bag with their price and sells one or all; a fish under the legal minimum length (species regulation data) is not bought and can be released; the tackle shop sells lures. The wallet (TL and fish in the bag) shows at the top of the world. Prices are design fills in `game/data/shop.json` (the file says so; no real prices were used). New pure `Economy` class; `Db.shop()`; `ShopPanel`; `WorldArea` shops and `WorldWalk.nearest_shop`; world debug menu gives +100 money, +3 fish, +5 lures. 184 tests.

- Roadmap R6 (most of it): what was drawn on the 2D overlay over the water is now part of the 3D scene. Rings, boils and the lure's ripple are drawn by the sea shader (up to 12, thin bands that push the surface normal, so they catch the sky and are hidden behind the rail like any real ripple). A fish under the water is a soft dark shadow along its spine in the sea shader (up to 8 blobs). A fish above the water, a jump or the landing and the lift on the line, is `Fish3D`: a lit silvery body with a dark back, a forked tail and an eye, built each frame from the spine (the lifted fish hangs from the rod tip in the camera's space at the tip's depth). Gulls are one `MultiMesh` draw call whose wings flap in a vertex shader (`gull.gdshader`); the 2D gull, fish and ring drawing is gone from the overlay. Still on the overlay by decision: the line (it is always in front of everything, so a mesh would add edge flicker and no depth benefit), splash droplets and the heading arrow. `WaterView.pack_rings`, `Fish3D.show_along` and `GullFlock.gull_transform` are tested. 170 tests.

- Fight completed (owner, 2026-11-15: finish every mechanics gap). Sideways runs: a running fish also goes left or right (an arrow at the line's end shows which way); sliding the thumb on Pull the other way is side strain, which holds the run and tires the fish faster. Jumps: a thrashing fish near the surface may jump; a hard rod pull during the jump (after 0.3 s to react) can throw the hook, so ease the rod and keep reeling; the "why" view names it. Landing is the player's: the fish hangs on the line and is reeled up to the rail; it kicks now and then, and reeling through a kick can shake it off (more easily where the landing chance is poor); the closed-form landing estimate now lies between careless and careful play. The "why" view shows the lure depth and speed the fish was actually hooked at instead of the fixed 3 m.
