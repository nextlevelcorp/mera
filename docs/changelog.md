# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Fight completed (owner, 2026-11-15: finish every mechanics gap). Sideways runs: a running fish also goes left or right (an arrow at the line's end shows which way); sliding the thumb on Pull the other way is side strain, which holds the run and tires the fish faster. Jumps: a thrashing fish near the surface may jump; a hard rod pull during the jump (after 0.3 s to react) can throw the hook, so ease the rod and keep reeling; the "why" view names it. Landing is the player's: the fish hangs on the line and is reeled up to the rail; it kicks now and then, and reeling through a kick can shake it off (more easily where the landing chance is poor); the closed-form landing estimate now lies between careless and careful play. The "why" view shows the lure depth and speed the fish was actually hooked at instead of the fixed 3 m. New design fills in `constants.json` (LATERAL_SHARE, SIDE_*, JUMP_*, LAND_KICK_*, LAND_HANG_HAZARD, LAND_BASE_SHARE). 133 tests.
- Decision: no anonymous counters (owner, 2026-11-15); `docs/counters_proposal.md` closed with option A.
- Retrieve and fight control (owner feedback 2026-11-15: casting and reeling felt like a rope, and the fight had no real pulling): the player now decides when the lure moves. The reel pad works during the wait: not reeling, a sinker sinks (count-down); reeling brings the lure in and lifts it; a diving lure dives deeper the faster it is reeled. Live depth and retrieve speed feed the catch model (stages 2 and 3), so working the lure at the right depth now matters, and a HUD line shows lure depth, water depth and reel speed. In the fight, pulling the rod or reeling gains line while the drag holds (`LIFT_M_PER_S`, `RISE_PER_M`, `DIVE_M_PER_S` design fills in `constants.json`); the legacy constant retrieve is kept for tests.
- Real time and place: with a save (the real game), the time-of-day slot, month and wind (direction and speed) now come from the phone's clock and a date-and-hour seed (`SessionConditions`, Istanbul poyraz and lodos weights as design fills); tests and headless runs keep the fixed evening in October. The debug menu has a Month button. 127 tests.
- Look and life (owner feedback 2026-11-15: the line looked like a rope; the others on the bridge did not feel like fishing alongside): the line is drawn as a thin thread, thicker near the rod and finer and fainter far out, with glints in the sun and a small ripple where it meets the water, and it hangs much tighter (less slack, a little wind belly). `NeighborAnglers`: four people along the rail, seen from behind with rods and lines, who jig, get nibbles, fight, lift fish (çapari rigs bring up two or three) and recast, at the pace of a bite at this place and hour, with a distant splash when one lifts a fish.
- Phone motion made more logical (owner: it worked but did not make sense): a flick back strikes only when a fish is on or just touched the lure; with nothing there it twitches the lure instead of the 'nothing there' penalty; hand tremor no longer counts as working the lure; a phone held still slowly makes its pose the rest pose; the rod on screen follows the phone's tilt at once, also before a cast. Settings now show a live view of tilt, speed, pull, lure work and strikes, a "set this pose as rest" button and a short explanation, so a wrong reading can be seen and reported. 114 tests.
- Docs: `docs/counters_proposal.md` (anonymous counters: options and a recommendation; GoatCounter's terms could not be read from this environment, so the hosted terms stay unverified; nothing added to the game).
- "Why?" view for the player (PRD F10, pillar 2): after every result a "Why?" button opens five plain lines from the model's stage numbers: depth (water depth, the species' feeding band, lure depth, fit), feeding time (activity in the hour's slot), tackle (lure size against the mouth, hook gape), line (peak tension against line strength, drag) and the reason for the outcome (line break, teeth, slack hook, the 90 s rule with the distance, lost at the edge). Fits are words (good, fair, poor), not colour. `WhyReport` is pure and unit-tested; a test checks every string's placeholders against its arguments in en, tr and es, and caught a wrong word order in the Turkish time line.

- Cast (owner feedback: cast, rod, reel and hands looked static): `CastMotion` swings the rod up and over the shoulder while charging and snaps it forward with an overshoot on release; the lure hangs from the tip and swings before the cast; the bail opens, line pays out from the spool during the flight and the bail snaps shut on landing. Thumb aim: sliding the thumb sideways while holding Cast aims, with a landing marker on the water (skill error added on release; owner choice).
