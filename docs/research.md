# Fishing game market: big audience, no proven niche

Status: phase 0 (Discovery) first pass, 2026-11-11. Evidence is weak (see verdict). Re-run recommended once blocked hosts are reachable.
> **Verdict: the evidence is WEAK.**
> - **Method limit.** All five researchers could only use web-search result summaries. WebFetch could not reach Steam, SteamDB, Reddit, Google Play, the App Store, fws.gov, pcgamer.com and others, so no primary page was read in full. Every figure is "reported by a search summary of the cited URL" and needs checking against the page before it goes into the PRD.
> - **Not found:** (1) no verified direct request from any player for a real-location shore-fishing game (0 instances); (2) no Turkish-language demand signal (0 instances). Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, but it is also not support.
> - **Corroborated** (several outlets agree): the commercial milestones of Dave the Diver, Dredge and Pokemon GO; Ten Square Games' reported Fishing Clash bookings (company-reported, seen via summary only).
> - **Single-source or aggregator estimates (do not quote as fact):** AppGoblin install and revenue figures, VaporLens sales estimates, Raijin sales and review snapshots, RoWatcher Roblox numbers, GameSensor, marketing-firm "fishing app" market sizes.
> - **Board:** 3 x FUND IF, average 4.7/10, resting on the same weak evidence.

## Research overview

**Question.** What do players of mobile and web fishing games, fishing sims and cozy games with fishing praise and complain about; what is unmet; and is there evidence of demand for a realistic, real-location shore-fishing game (project Mera)?

**Method.** Five researchers each covered one slice: mobile and browser games, PC and console simulators, cozy/indie/RPG titles, market economics, and real-location demand (Turkey-weighted). A further step, a three-member founder board (Offers, Monopoly, Product lenses), read the brief and voted. Access date for every source: 2026-11-11.

**Limits, stated plainly.**
- Search summaries only. No verbatim, dated, attributable player review could be verified, so this report paraphrases. Quotes appear only where an outlet or developer statement was relayed by the search summary and the headline or quote is named; they are outlet quotes, not verified player quotes.
- Player voice is anecdotal and critic voice is over-represented (especially for Moonglow Bay). Counted review themes do not exist; "frequency" below means the number of distinct games or sources where a theme appeared, not review counts.
- Not covered at all: Legendary Fishing, Rapala Fishing Pro Series, Pro Fishing Simulator, Reel Fishing, Spiritfarer, Cozy Grove, Disney Dreamlight Valley, Hello Kitty Island Adventure, Fishing Strike, Fishing Paradise 3D, Monster Fishing, Fishing Life. Reddit, Eksi Sozluk, Donanimhaber, YouTube and Instagram were not reached.
- Confidence labels: **High** = several independent outlets agree on a fact; **Medium** = two or three sources, some secondary; **Low** = one source, an aggregator, or developer marketing.

## Key findings

### 1. Fishing-loop games sell at large scale, but the winners are hybrids, and the live-service tier is shrinking
- **Evidence.** Dave the Diver reached 1M copies about 10 days after launch ([Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/indie-title-dave-the-diver-sells-one-million-copies-in-10-days-520838)), 5M by November 2024 ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2024/11/dave-the-diver-sales-top-five-million)) and 10M reported in September 2026 ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/09/beloved-undersea-adventure-dave-the-diver-has-sold-a-whopping-10-million-copies)). Dredge passed 1M copies about seven months after its March 2023 launch, against an internal hope of about 100,000 in year one ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/dredge-smashes-internal-expectations-after-topping-1-million-sales); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/an-indie-game-dredge-has-sold-over-1-million-copies/)). Fishing Clash bookings were PLN 197.5M in 2025, down 18.6% y/y; these are bookings, not recognised revenue ([Ten Square Games FY2025](https://tensquaregames.com/2026/03/23/financial-results-of-ten-square-games-group-for-q4-and-full-year-2025-trophy-hunter-and-portfolio-transformation-mark-a-new-stage-of-development/)).
- **Frequency.** Three titles with multi-outlet agreement.
- **Impact.** Shows a paying audience exists for fishing-flavoured games. Dave the Diver is a diving and sushi-management hybrid and Dredge is a horror-mystery game; the notes say their upside comes from hooks beyond fishing. Neither is a realistic sim.
- **Confidence: High** for the milestones; **Low** for the inference that they transfer to a calm realistic RPG.

### 2. Monetisation friction is the dominant complaint in mobile and F2P fishing games
- **Evidence.** Pay-to-win or paying for lures: Fishing Clash ([Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-fishing-clash-customer-feedback-report-app-store-us-144588)), Fishing Rival 3D ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/fishing-rival-3d)), Fishing Planet ([Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/380600/fishing_planet.md)). Energy timers: Fishing Master ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/fishing-master)), Fishing Travel ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/fishing-travel)). Ads: Let's Fish ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/lets-fish)), Ace Angler ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/ace-angler-fishing-spirits-m/reviews)), Hooked Inc ([Instalki](https://www.instalki.pl/download/android/gry/symulacyjne/hooked-inc-fishing-games/)). On PC, Russian Fishing 4's Mixed rating is attributed to its $2,000 "lifetime subscription" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/this-russian-fishing-sim-is-bizarrely-popular-on-steam-but-players-cant-decide-if-they-love-or-hate-it-perhaps-because-of-the-usd2000-microtransaction)). Inversely, the absence of P2W and intrusive ads is praised for Ace Fishing Crew ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/ace-fishing-crew-real-fishing)) and Russian Fishing 4 ([Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/766570/russian_fishing_4)).
- **Frequency.** At least 7 games across 3 complaint types. Every source is an aggregator or auto-summary of reviews; per-review counts are unknown.
- **Impact.** A no-ads, no-timers, no-P2W promise is a credible differentiator on paper, but it also removes the monetisation that funds the incumbents. Fishing Clash is estimated (by AppGoblin, unreliable) at a 65% ads / 35% IAP split ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1151811380/trends)).
- **Confidence: Medium.**

### 3. The skill-test input layer of fishing minigames polarises players
- **Evidence.** Stardew Valley's creator said it is controversial and that many people hate it, and regretted that it starts too hard ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/life-sim/a-lot-of-people-hate-it-but-i-think-its-fun-eric-barone-backs-stardew-valleys-fishing-minigame-but-does-admit-that-it-starts-too-hard); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/concernedape-regrets-making-stardew-valleys-fishing-minigame-too-hard-when-you-start-out-but-still-thinks-its-good-overall-i-know-its-controversial)). A "Skip Fishing Minigame" mod is reported at almost 2.5M Nexus downloads; this figure is from the search summary of the PC Gamer article and unverified. Forum commenters are split between "peak minigame design" and clunky bar acceleration ([Stardew forums](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/why-do-so-many-people-hate-fishing.55483/latest)). Bassmaster 2022 critics said realism attempts clash with arcade elements ([Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/bassmaster-fishing-2022-super-deluxe-edition/)).
- **Frequency.** 3+ independent instances, all in Stardew coverage plus one sim.
- **Impact.** Directly relevant to Pillar 4: the fight must be short and intense but must not be a fatiguing skill gate. The complaint is about the input layer, not about fishing.
- **Confidence: Medium** (the mod download count is Low until verified).

### 4. Repetition and monotony hit both slow cozy loops and realistic sims
- **Evidence.** Moonglow Bay drew "repetitive" verdicts from Cubed3 (4/10), Explosion Network, TechRaptor and The Xbox Hub ([Cubed3](https://www.cubed3.com/games/reviews/pc/moonglow-bay); [Explosion Network](https://explosionnetwork.com/moonglow-bay-review); [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/moonglow-bay-review); [The Xbox Hub](https://www.thexboxhub.com/moonglow-bay-review/)), yet RPGamer called the loop addictive ([RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/review/moonglow-bay-review/)). Fishing Sim World was "monotonous at times" ([PCInvasion](https://www.pcinvasion.com/?p=186904)). Cast n Chill's review summary lists limited content and repetition ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/3483740/cast_n_chill/stats/details)). Long waits for bites are a frustration in Russian Fishing 4 ([Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/766570/russian_fishing_4)). Hooked Inc progress is called slow and repetitive ([MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/?p=576948)). Webfishing peaked at 24,085 concurrent players on 2024-11-07 and sat at roughly 150-300 in mid-to-late 2026 snapshots ([SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/3146520/charts); [Steambase](https://steambase.io/apps/webfishing)); one tracker (Raijin) shows a conflicting 752 all-time peak and is treated as an outlier.
- **Frequency.** 6+ games.
- **Impact.** The board flagged the same risk (cast-bite-fight may not stay interesting). The Call of the Wild director said real fishing is mostly waiting and that realism has to give way to fun ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/reeling-in-players-with-the-design-of-call-of-the-wild-angler)). Faithful waiting is unfun unless waiting itself is made rewarding (inference).
- **Confidence: Medium.**

### 5. Realism is praised where tackle, bait, weather and environment visibly matter; fish-behaviour realism is mostly developer marketing
- **Evidence.** Euro Fishing is marketed as the closest to real fishing from a sofa, with carp experts consulted (publisher claim) ([CARPology](https://www.carpology.net/article/dovetail-games-euro-fishing)); a review notes the focus on wind, rain, bait and tactics ([Xbox Hub](https://www.thexboxhub.com/euro-fishing-ultimate-edition-review/)). Bassmaster 2022 was read as an in-depth tournament simulation but lacking flair for wider gamers ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/12306/bassmaster-fishing-2022/reviews)). Fishing Planet claims behaviour depends on season, weather, time, current and bottom type ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1542220472)) while one Steam commenter says it has no realistic biting or fish behaviour ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408610/discussions/0/5260782129625906491)). iFishing reviewers like that location, lure, depth and reel speed change catches ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/i-fishing/id294380152)); one reviewer wanted per-lake scenery, a matching sounder, and no 50 ft fight limit. A Fly Fishing Simulator reviewer said fights last far too long and fish never tire ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id886195140?l=en-us)).
- **Frequency.** 5 games, each 1-2 sources; line-tension praise: no evidence.
- **Impact.** Supports Pillar 2 as a direction (depth, feeding time, tackle match readable on screen). It does not show that players pay for it.
- **Confidence: Low.**

### 6. Real places are used as a marketing pillar, but no player was found asking for real locations or shore fishing
- **Evidence.** Pro Fishing Simulator says its 9 areas are based on real places ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/794180/PRO_FISHING_SIMULATOR/)); Real VR Fishing advertises 40 maps growing to 95 spots ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1266470)); Fishing Planet says waterways are based on real locations ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/380600)); Euro Fishing offers five European venues fished from pegs and banks ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/sub/440493)). Player-side: a Steam thread titled "To the Devs: The honest issues with this game from real life angler's perspective" exists for Fishing Planet ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/380600/discussions/0/528398719797653719)), another player calls it a "recipe" game, and Fishing Sim World forum users asked for more shoreline structure ([Dovetail forum](https://forums.dovetailgames.com/threads/a-few-things.15096/)). All thread contents are search-summary paraphrase; dates unknown.
- **Frequency.** Direct requests for a real-location shore game: **0 verified**. Adjacent angler critiques: 3 threads.
- **Impact.** The central Mera claim is unevidenced as demand. Euro Fishing is the nearest shore-peg competitor.
- **Confidence: Low.**

### 7. Real-place anchoring scales in social and exploration products, which is not the same as demand for a simulation
- **Evidence.** Fishbrain grew from about 430,000 registered users at its 2014 round to over 5M members at its 2018 $13.5M Series B ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/03/07/fishbrain-the-fishing-app-and-social-network-raises-13-5m-series-b); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20180307005699/en/Fishbrain-Raises-Series-13.5-Million-Led-Capital)). Later user claims conflict: 11M+ per Wikipedia versus 14M+ registered per a company release, dates unclear and registered not active ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishbrain); [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/fishbrain-to-raise-through-investment-platform-republic-301582671.html)). Pokemon GO had about 20M weekly and 10M daily players in March 2025 ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokmon-go-has-20m-weekly-active-players-nearly-nine-years-after-its-global-launch)).
- **Frequency.** 2 products, both non-simulation.
- **Impact.** Anglers adopt phone apps tied to real waters; a spot-sourced game could borrow that trust. It is an inference, not demand.
- **Confidence: Low.**

### 8. Cozy is booming as a label, with crowded launches and unproven sales
- **Evidence.** The share of successful new Steam games calling themselves cozy rose from 0.4% (2022) to 3.1% (2025) per GameDiscoverCo via PC Gamer ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/life-sim/the-cozy-game-boom-is-the-clearest-trend-on-steam-over-five-years-of-data)); a counter-analysis says the surge reflects more releases and marketing with sales unproven ([MacMyths](https://macmyths.com/are-we-in-the-cozy-gaming-gold-rush-era-steams-cozy-tag-is-surging-but-sales-are-unproven/)). Cast n Chill (2025-06-16) is rated about 95% positive on one tracker (5,603 reviews; another lists 4,823 English of 7,820 total), OpenCritic average 86 ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/18729/cast-n-chill); [VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/3483740/cast_n_chill/stats/details)); its sales (about 582,000 copies, about $8.7M) are a VaporLens estimate only ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/3483740/cast_n_chill.md)). Steam Fishing Fest 2025 featured almost 200 fishing titles ([Simulation Daily](https://simulationdaily.com/news/steam-fishing-fest-2025/)), and several 2026 fishing-centred titles are listed (e.g. [Pier Pals](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4643120/Pier_Pals/), [Fish & Hike](https://www.gamesunit.de/artikel/cast-your-lines-fish-hike-launches-into-early-access-on-steam-on-june-4-2026-232032.html)); their sales are unknown.
- **Frequency.** Several sources; the sales link is disputed.
- **Impact.** Discovery, not feasibility, is the likely constraint; the board flagged the same crowding.
- **Confidence: Medium** for crowding; **Low** for cozy-premium willingness to pay (a paid-cozy median list price of USD 4.99 comes from a low-confidence tool, [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/cozy)).

## Segments

**Realism hobbyists (real anglers who play sims).** Evidence: Steam angler-perspective threads, Euro Fishing's carp-expert positioning, Russian Fishing 4's large audience. They want tackle match, structure and believable fish behaviour. Risk: they may prefer fishing itself, and monetisation and monotony drive their complaints. Size for shore anglers: unknown. There is no shore or bank share figure in any country, and the only headcounts are US 39.9M anglers aged 16+ in 2022 ([FWS summary via Guidefitter](https://guidefitter.com/blog/2022-us-fws-survey-fishing-hunting-wildlife-watching-trends); the primary report was not fetched), UK 551,000 to 823,000 sea anglers depending on survey period and method ([Defra/MMO 2016-17](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/896908/UK_Sea_Angling_2016-17_exec_summary_final.pdf); [2018-19 report](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/1004226/Sea_angling__2018-19_report.pdf)), and for Turkey about 2M rod anglers per the Türkiye Olta Balıkçılığı Federasyonu, an advocacy estimate with no stated date and no registration ([Mynet](https://www.mynet.com/turkiyede-2-milyona-yakin-olta-balikcisi-var-180101228739)). A 2014 SUBIS count of 210,931 amateur certificates is a floor from when the certificate was not compulsory ([TRDizin](https://app.trdizin.gov.tr//makale/TXpJeE5Ea3hNUT09)). A defensible Turkish range is therefore about 0.2M to 2M, with the upper end unverified.

**Cozy collectors.** Evidence: Cast n Chill, Moonglow Bay, Stardew, Animal Crossing's recurring fishing tourney ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2020/04/reminder_animal_crossing_new_horizons_fishing_tourney_starts_today)). They want calm and low input pressure; many skip the minigame. Collection and log-completion appeal is only indirectly evidenced (THIN).

**Casual mobile and browser players.** Evidence: Fishing Clash, Hooked Inc, Tiny Fishing. Short sessions, idle or arcade mechanics. A deep RPG with long sessions fits ad-funded portal economics poorly (inference; portal audience claims of 35M+ monthly users for CrazyGames and 50M+ for Poki are unaudited, [Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-web-game)).

**Turkish shore anglers.** Evidence: none for games. Turkish game portals list Fishing Planet, Rapala and Ultimate Fishing Simulator as the realistic options ([Tamindir](https://www.tamindir.com/windows/fishing-planet/)); Turkish search surfaced no real-location shore title ([Tamindir list](https://www.tamindir.com/liste/en-iyi-balik-tutma-oyunlari/); [Gaming.net TR](https://www.gaming.net/tr/best-fishing-games/)). That is a gap in the search, not proof of a gap in the market.

## Opportunity areas

1. **Readable realism with a fair, short fight.** Complaints about fake fight length and the "recipe" feel sit beside praise for visible tackle and weather effects. Evidence: Low to Medium.
2. **Trust-based sourcing.** Competitors market real places but I found no one labelling sourced facts versus design fill. Whether players care is untested.
3. **No ads, no timers, no P2W.** Documented complaint cluster (Finding 2), but it conflicts with how the largest title earns.
4. **Input without a skill gate.** The Stardew mod suggests a many-want-the-outcome-without-the-bar audience (mod figure unverified).
5. **Offline, single-player economics.** Live-service fishing shutdowns cite revenue not covering ongoing cost: Fishing Frenzy ([PlayToEarn](https://playtoearn.com/news/fishing-frenzy-and-developer-uncharted-shut-down-as-crypto-gaming-bet-ends)), World of Fishing ([Steam community](https://steamcommunity.com/app/421960/discussions/0/1290691937707991712)), Atom Fishing 2 ([Delisted Games](https://delistedgames.com/atom-fishing-2/)). A no-network design avoids that cost (inference).

## Recommendations

1. **Treat this as a hypothesis, not a validated market.** Recommended: run a cheap demand test before more building (Option A below).
2. **Re-run the research with network access** to store.steampowered.com, steamdb.info, steamcommunity.com, reddit.com, pcgamer.com, play.google.com, apps.apple.com, fws.gov, dergipark.org.tr and Turkish forums, then re-check every number marked estimate.
3. **Pick one first audience** before design work (see the two-audience risk below).
4. **Do not quote any aggregator estimate** (AppGoblin, VaporLens, Raijin, RoWatcher, GameSensor) in the PRD or store copy.
5. **Collect the missing evidence directly:** talk to 5 anglers and 5 non-anglers (the board's pass-bar test), and look for Turkish-language threads on olta forums and Eksi Sozluk.

## Board stress test

The founder board (Offers, Monopoly and Product lenses, applying published frameworks, no real person's views) read only the brief built from these same notes. Vote: **3 x FUND IF** (no fund, no pass). Scores: Offers 4, Monopoly 4, Product 6; **average 4.7/10**. Because the board saw only the weak-evidence brief, its verdict is a stress test of the idea's logic, not independent validation.

Risks raised by more than one member:
- No verified demand for a real-location shore game and no Turkish-language demand signal (all three).
- Possibly two audiences with opposite wants: realism hobbyist vs cozy collector.
- The cast-bite-fight loop may become repetitive, like disliked minigames and "monotonous" realistic sims.
- No distribution plan or cost per player in a crowded field of fishing launches.

Single-member risks: realism and sourced data are copyable with no durable edge; phone web performance and landscape-only could break the first five minutes.

Merged conditions: name one first audience and a one-sentence pitch; show a verified demand signal in at least one language market (waitlist or priced pre-sale); build a one-spot slice and test with at least 5 anglers and 5 non-anglers against a pass bar set beforehand, including whether the fight stays interesting across repeated catches; measure frame rate on a named mid-range phone; name one distribution channel and measure cost per player; name the compounding asset (sourced data, community or brand) and keep the no-ads, no-timers promise; set a stop date if tests fail. The strongest version the board can see is a free, calm, no-ads web game with one very good real Turkish spot, aimed at one audience, used as a cheap experiment before widening.

## What this means for the spec

Nothing here is decided for the owner. Each tension lists options and a recommendation.

**1. Scope: 3-5 spots and 15-25 species MVP vs the board's one-spot, test-first path.** The board's conditions favour a smaller slice; the spec's MVP is larger, and the research gives no evidence that breadth is what players want. Options: (A) keep the spec MVP and test later; (B) build one spot first, test, then widen; (C) one spot plus a priced waitlist in parallel. **Recommendation: B, with the C waitlist, because it matches phase 3's own vertical-slice rule and the evidence is too weak to justify five spots.** This touches scope, so it needs the owner's go-ahead.

**2. Two-audience risk.** Realism hobbyists and cozy collectors want different things (fight depth and tackle logic vs low input pressure). Options: (A) anglers first; (B) cozy first with realism as a quiet depth layer; (C) both, accepting the risk. **Recommendation: pick one for the test; no evidence in the notes favours either, so let the 5-plus-5 test pick it.**

**3. Unproven claims.** Pillars 1 and 2 are design choices, not demonstrated demand: no verified request for real locations, no counted praise for line tension, no learning-value evidence, no Turkish signal. Options: (A) proceed on belief; (B) run the waitlist or pre-sale test first; (C) both. **Recommendation: B before further gameplay investment; phase 1 is not yet approved in any case.**

**4. OSM ODbL licence decision (needs the owner).** An OSM-based game is generally a "produced work" needing visible attribution; share-alike can attach if OSM data is enriched into a derivative database or substantial parts are recreated, and "substantial" is undefined ([OSM wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/License/Use_Cases); [OSMF FAQ](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence_and_Legal_FAQ); [OSM legal-talk 2021](https://lists.openstreetmap.org/pipermail/legal-talk/2021-February/009056.html); [LWG minutes 2025-11-10](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licensing_Working_Group/Minutes/2025-11-10)). Shipping per-spot data files in the public web export is the scenario most likely to trigger share-alike (the researcher's inference; the OSMF pages were not opened). Options: (A) hand-author spots from non-OSM sources; (B) keep OSM-derived data in a separate attributed file; (C) accept ODbL share-alike for the spot data; (D) take legal advice first. **Recommendation: A for the first spot, plus D before any OSM use.** Fictional tackle names, labelled design fill, avoid the brand-licensing question; real-brand games are licensed ([MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/game/87200/); [Rapala](https://www.rapala.com/us_en/blog/rapala-fishing-sim-your-newest-app-addiction)). Fish data licences (FishBase, GBIF, IUCN) were not researched.

**5. Business model and platform.** No evidence that web or mobile players pay premium prices for fishing sims, and the only realistic-sim sales datapoint is an unofficial estimate of about $2.5M gross for a mixed-reviewed, well-funded title. Portal revenue splits are conflicting secondary claims: Poki reported as 50/50 on its traffic ([Playgama](https://playgama.com/blog/business-faqs/poki-vs-crazygames-vs-gamedistribution-revenue-share/)), CrazyGames 55% to 60% of ads depending on source ([Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames)). Portal exclusivity could conflict with a later native release. Options: free with a paid unlock; premium on Steam and itch.io; ad-funded portal. **Recommendation: defer; it needs the owner's decision and the real developer agreements.**

## Open questions

- Do shore anglers, in Turkey or elsewhere, ask for or play a real-location game? Needs Reddit, Eksi Sozluk, Donanimhaber, olta forums and YouTube.
- What share of anglers fish from shore, per country? No figure found.
- What do Steam negative reviews of Euro Fishing, Fishing Planet and Call of the Wild actually say, by date?
- Do cozy players value collection and log completion in fishing games? Indirect evidence only.
- Is retention in calm fishing games healthy? Webfishing's decay is a caution; no D1/D7/D30 benchmarks were found.
- How many fishing games shipped on Steam in 2023-2026, and how did they sell? Not obtained.
- What regional rules apply (age rating, Turkey and Spain specifics, children's data)? Not researched.
- Which fish-data licence applies to species lists? Not researched.

## Conclusion

The research shows a large, paying audience for fishing in games and a loud list of things it dislikes: pay-to-win, timers, gating minigames and repetition. It does not show that anyone is asking for what Mera is. The commercial winners are hybrids, the one realistic-sim sales datapoint is modest and unofficial, and the real-place products that scale are social apps. Mera's pillars read as a sound answer to documented complaints, but each pillar remains an informed bet.

The useful next move is evidence, not more design: a priced or waitlist test in one language market, five anglers and five non-anglers on a one-spot slice, and a re-run of this research with the blocked hosts open. Until then the board's 4.7/10 is the right reading: fund only against conditions, with a stop date.
